## 1. Purpose

Outline how Autlantic processes personal data on behalf of the merchant in connection with Autlantic Billing (hosted API, merchant portal, webhooks, and related support).

## 2. Roles

- Merchant determines purposes and means for end-customer / member data it submits.
- Autlantic processes that data to provide Billing services under the merchant’s instructions and Autlantic’s product Terms / Privacy Policy.

## 3. Categories of data (typical)

Examples only; actual scope depends on what the merchant sends:

- Account and contact identifiers (email, name, merchant refs)
- Billing metadata (subscription and invoice identifiers, amounts, statuses)
- Wallet / payout addresses and on-chain transaction references
- Webhook delivery and support correspondence

Autlantic Billing settles USDC on Base to merchant-configured addresses and does not custody merchant subscription revenue.

## 4. Processing instructions

Process only as needed to provide the service, comply with law, and as documented in product Terms, Privacy Policy, and written instructions from the merchant (including API and portal usage).

## 5. Security

Technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service (access control, secrets handling, transport encryption, webhook signing). Integrator obligations remain as documented in the [Security guide](/guide/security), [Security FAQ](https://autlantic.com/security), and repository `SECURITY.md`.

## 6. Subprocessors

Use of infrastructure subprocessors as disclosed on the canonical [autlantic.com/dpa](https://autlantic.com/dpa) schedule and summarized on the [Trust center](/guide/trust). Do not treat this docs page as the authoritative list. Notice / objection mechanics to be agreed in the final DPA.

## 7. International transfers

Transfer mechanisms (where applicable) to be completed by counsel for the merchant’s jurisdiction.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Retain for the life of the service relationship and as required for legal, security, and accounting purposes; deletion or return on request subject to backup and legal holds. Details to be finalized in the executed DPA.

## 9. Assistance

Reasonable assistance with data subject requests, security incidents, and audits as agreed in the final DPA and subject to confidentiality and cost provisions.

## 10. Liability and precedence

Commercial Terms, Privacy Policy, and the signed DPA control. This page has no contractual effect.
